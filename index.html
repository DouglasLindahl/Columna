02 — What Columna does

Good ideas grow stronger with the right structure around them.

Columna Group works alongside owners and entrepreneurs to build businesses with clarity, strength and direction.

Together, we create practical structures around ownership, responsibilities, agreements, finances and decision-making.

Some businesses begin within the group. Others are developed together with people who bring industry knowledge, commitment and the ambition to build something that lasts.

Our objective is simple: every business should grow stronger through clear structure and shared support.

The experience behind Columna

Experience shapes how we build.

Columna Group was founded by Scott Lindahl, drawing on more than three decades of experience in building, operating and developing businesses across several industries and international markets.

This background has shaped a practical and adaptable approach — grounded in Swedish business understanding and strengthened by perspectives from different countries, cultures and ways of working.

Today, Columna grows through the people, partners and entrepreneurs who bring their knowledge, commitment and perspective to the ecosystem.

03 — Partnerships

We work with people who know their industries.

Columna partners with entrepreneurs and specialists who understand their field and bring the interest, commitment and drive to lead the day-to-day business.

Some partnerships begin with an established business. Others begin with an idea from Columna and the right person to take the baton and carry it forward.

Columna strengthens the structure around the business — ownership, agreements, financial understanding, business models and long-term direction — together with access to experience, networks and support throughout the ecosystem.

A strong partnership begins with clarity: who owns what, who decides what, what each person contributes and how the value created is shared.

When a new business grows from a Columna idea, shared ownership may form part of the conversation. A clear foundation allows everyone to focus on their strengths and helps the business grow with confidence.

05 — Our Philosophy

Shared strength creates lasting businesses.

A sustainable business turns the knowledge and experience of individuals into strength shared across the organisation.

Owners, managers, salespeople, administrators and specialists often carry essential knowledge, customer relationships and responsibilities. Bringing that value into the wider organisation creates continuity and confidence.

Resilience grows as knowledge is shared, working methods are documented, responsibilities are distributed and people within the organisation are given room to develop.

In some businesses, additional people bring the capacity and specialist knowledge that support the next stage of development.

Important knowledge, customer relationships and decisions become part of the business and form a foundation that continues to grow.

The people remain central. Their knowledge and experience gain even greater value when they strengthen the entire company.
